# بخش ۴ — سبک بصری (Style Bible)

**Genre:** Commercial drama, subtle humor, premium tech ad finish  
**Color grade:** Act 1 neutral-warm · Act 2 restaurant clean daylight · Act 3 purple-navy gradient (#8E6AFE / #102A56) for product montage  
**Camera:** Mostly stable; slight handheld only on stressed man shots  
**Lens feel:** 35mm documentary · close-ups 85mm shallow depth  
**Avoid (Negative prompt global):** cartoon, anime, CGI plastic skin, extra fingers, distorted faces, unreadable text on screens, watermark, logo of other brands, violence, romantic kiss, alcohol bottles prominent, night club

**Negative prompt (copy):**  
`cartoon, anime, illustration, deformed hands, blurry face, low quality, watermark, text gibberish, wrong logos, oversaturated, horror, kiss, drunk`
