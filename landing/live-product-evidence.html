2. What “live” means here

  • Hosted web application with authentication and plan/role gates (Supabase-backed).
  • Working modules: unit grids, sales/reservations, installments, project finance views, owner portal, inventory, maps, bilingual EN/FA UI, installable PWA.
  • Founder runs a live construction project on the product (domain validation).
  • Two co-founders + four operator reviewers; labeled demo account for evaluators (sample data, not paying customers).

3. Why this is not “local services”

  • Multi-tenant SaaS on annual plans — same product for many customers.
  • Growth by adding subscribers, not by staffing a custom build per construction site.
  • International path: learn in a familiar market → Estonia / EU company and outbound sales → broader Europe.

3. Demo access (private)

Do not publish the demo password in public PDFs or git. Email for the labeled demo: demo@metrico.app. Password: provide only in a private note field of the application form or a secure message to the programme. See companion PDF metrico-demo-access-en.pdf for the print layout used in prior packs.

4. Honest traction stance

Early-stage validation: founder-as-user, small feedback circle, live product. No fake logos, no invented MRR. Commercial scale and EU card billing are on the roadmap after company formation — disclosed in the due-diligence summary.
