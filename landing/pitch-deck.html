01 / 10 · Problem

The daily mess on mid-size builds

Units, buyers, and money live in three different places.

On mid-size residential and commercial projects, the pre-sale → build → handover loop still runs across spreadsheets, chat threads, and paper. The people who need answers lose hours stitching them together.

  • Unit status, buyers, and installment schedules sit in Excel, messaging apps, and notebooks — rarely in one calm view.
  • Site teams need tools that still work when connectivity is weak.
  • Owners ask for progress; the full ledger has to stay private.
  • Generic CRMs track contacts. Heavy construction suites chase job-site logistics. Neither owns the unit + installment + site-finance reality of mid-size developers.

02 / 10 · Solution

One operating loop

A bilingual field app for units, money, and owners.

Metrico is a live SaaS / PWA built for that loop — so a developer or site supervisor can see sold units, what is owed, and what an owner can safely see, without rebuilding the same report every week.

  • Visual floor / unit map: sold, reserved, vacant — not only tables.
  • Contract math from m² × price, remaining debt, average sale price per m².
  • Installments, project costs, investor shares, check & cash reports, PDF exports.
  • Owner portal with a progress gallery — updates without exposing the books.
  • Installable offline-capable PWA for weak-signal sites. English and Persian by design.

07 / 10 · Differentiation

Why operators reach for it

The Swiss-army knife for the construction site.

Not a slogan factory. When units, money, and owners collide on one project, mid-size teams need one calm tool — not three half-fitted systems and a spreadsheet that never quite matches.

  • All-in-one site ops for the pre-sale → handover money loop: visual unit grids, installment math, site finance, owner updates.
  • Construction-native objects out of the box — floors, units, sale status, installments, ledgers, PDF reports — instead of forcing a generic CRM to pretend it knows a unit map.
  • Field-first delivery: bilingual UI, offline-capable PWA, owner-safe sharing without opening the full books.
  • Depth when needed: drawings in-app, maps with market signal, contracts & QC, takeoff and energy on higher plans.

Positioning in one line: credible field utility for mid-size construction — not a gimmick brand.
