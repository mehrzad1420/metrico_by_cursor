3. Not a local service business

  • Product: one multi-tenant SaaS codebase.
  • Revenue: annual subscriptions (Starter free → paid tiers), not hourly consulting.
  • Delivery: customers self-serve in the app; we do not rebuild custom software per site.
  • Growth: add customers at hosting/support cost — not linear headcount per project.

4. International scale path

  • ~30% effort: familiar market — learn workflows (not the end destination).
  • ~50% effort: Estonia / EU — company, hire, outbound SaaS sales.
  • ~20% effort: later markets; German / DACH localization next after EN.
  • Same product core across countries; localization and billing layers expand reach.

5. Benefit to the Estonian economy (forward-looking, honest)

  • Register an Estonian company and operate Metrico as EU-based SaaS (contracts, compliance, billing from Estonia).
  • Hire at least one software engineer in Estonia / EU — real employment, not freelancing-as-visa theatre.
  • Export multi-tenant software from Estonia to construction teams in multiple countries.
  • Pay taxes and contribute skills inside Estonia’s digital / startup ecosystem.
