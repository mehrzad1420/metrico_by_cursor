Metrico

Construction command center — units, money, owners

Live SaaS / PWA
EN · FA
One-pager rev 2

Metrico replaces spreadsheet chaos for mid-size developers and contractors: unit sales, installment schedules, project finance, and owner updates in one bilingual offline-capable app.

Problem

  • Unit status, buyers, and installments live in Excel, chat, and paper.
  • Site teams need mobile tools that work with weak connectivity.
  • Owners ask for progress; full ledgers must stay private.
  • Generic CRMs and project tools miss pre-sale + installment reality.

Solution

  • Visual floor / unit map (not only tables): sold, reserved, vacant.
  • Auto math: contract from m² × price, remaining debt, avg sale price / m².
  • Installments, project costs, investor shares, check & cash reports, PDFs.
  • Owner portal + progress gallery — updates without exposing full ledgers.
  • Installable PWA with offline sync for weak-signal sites.

Differentiators vs generic CRM / project tools

  • Pre-sale → handover in one stack — units, buyers, installments, site finance, owner comms.
  • In-app drawings — DWG / DXF / PDF / BIM-style files viewed inside the product (no export hop).
  • Maps with market signal — project pins + KML export including avg sale price / m² by site.
  • Takeoff & energy (Ark) — 5-category material estimate, map digitizer, building energy tools.
  • Contracts & QC (Pro) — contractor agreements, progress statements, activity-linked quality checks.
  • Inventory + tax prep (Pro) — materials stock / allocations; tax-system readiness path.
  • Privacy-minded assistant — rule-based answers on your data; chat not sent to external LLMs.
  • Bilingual by design — English + Persian (RTL), built for cross-border teams — not a bolted-on translate.

Why this is a startup (not a local service business)

  • Repeatable SaaS — same product sold on annual plans, not custom agency work.
  • Software leverage — growth via product, not linear headcount per project.
  • Domain wedge — purpose-built for construction pre-sale → build → handover.
  • Cross-border ready — bilingual UI; EU company base and EU billing on the roadmap.

Product stage

  • Live application in production use for demos and onboarding.
  • Four plans: Starter (free) → Plus → Pro → Ark.
  • Labeled demo account for evaluators (sample data, not traction).
  • Higher tiers: inventory, contracts, takeoff, energy tools.

Market & GTM

  • Wedge: mid-size developers drowning in unit + installment ops.
  • Early market: learn from real projects where the pain is acute.
  • Expansion: Turkey, Gulf, then European adopters.
  • Base: incorporate and operate from Estonia / EU.

Business model

  • B2B subscription, annual billing.
  • EU list: €50 / €100 / €150 per year (Plus / Pro / Ark).
  • Local checkout in progress; EU card billing under development.
  • Land free → expand projects and modules as portfolios grow.

Estonia fit

We seek Startup Committee recognition to build Metrico as an Estonian / EU SaaS company: hire specialized engineering and GTM talent, complete EU-ready billing and compliance, and use Estonia’s digital infrastructure as the operating base — while expanding into international construction markets. Iran (or any single early market) is a learning wedge, not the end state.
