Why this team fits the product

Construction unit sales and installment finance fail when product design ignores field reality or accounting practice. Metrico pairs a builder/operator founder with a practicing construction-finance co-founder (consultant/analyst) so the SaaS encodes both site workflows and money workflows — not a local consultancy stack rebuilt per project.
